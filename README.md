# Your Pension and the Triple Lock

A simple guide for pensioners to the planned change to the State Pension triple lock from April 2030, with a calculator showing rises in pounds per week.

Live site: https://hannah-o-rourke.github.io/triple-lock-guide/

The calculator follows the new rule as described by the Institute for Fiscal Studies: each year the pension rises by the highest of CPI inflation, 2.5%, or the amount needed to keep up with average earnings growth since the new rule began. The examples are illustrative, not forecasts.
