Commentary

This is a made-up example over 20 years, with a jump in prices and a slow patch for pay. It is not a forecast. It shows how the rules work.

What the race shows

🛒
The pension always beats prices.Every year it goes up by at least as much as prices, and at least 2.5%. So what your pension can buy keeps growing.
👷
The pension keeps level with workers' pay.Under the new rule, pensions grow as much as pay has grown since 2030. If prices jump, pensions go up straight away. Then they wait for pay to catch up. If pay falls behind, pensions still go up 2.5%.
🧓
Under the old rule, pensions run off ahead of pay.The old rule takes the biggest rise each year. After a jump in prices, it pays out twice: once for prices, then again when pay catches up. Over time that gets more and more costly. Try the "Old triple lock" button to see it.
